# Compounding Knowledge

**Compounding Knowledge** is the property of a knowledge repository where each new source or insight enriches and updates the existing web of understanding rather than sitting in an isolated silo.

---

## Mechanism in LLM Wikis
In a compounding system:
1. **Incremental Integration**: New sources do not merely get indexed; they update existing entity and concept pages.
2. **Cross-Referencing**: Associative trails connect disparate domains over time.
3. **Contradiction Resolution**: As claims evolve, conflicts between past knowledge and new discoveries are surfaced and reconciled.
4. **Query Feedback Loop**: Insights generated during ad-hoc queries are written back to the wiki as new synthesis or comparison notes.

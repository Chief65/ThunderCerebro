# Source: LLM Wiki Idea Manifest

**Original Document**: `raw/llm-wiki-idea.md`  
**Core Theme**: Transforming LLM knowledge management from stateless chunk retrieval (RAG) into a compiled, persistent, LLM-maintained second brain.

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Traditional Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) rediscovers relationships from scratch on every query with zero accumulation. The **LLM Wiki** pattern introduces an intermediate compiled markdown layer where an LLM agent continuously curates, cross-links, updates, and lints a persistent knowledge graph.

## 2. Key Insights
- **Persistent Compounding**: Knowledge is compiled once and continuously updated rather than re-derived on every question.
- **Role Division**: The human curates sources and directs exploration; the LLM handles all bookkeeping, linking, summarizing, and contradiction resolution.
- **Mental Model**: **[[wiki/entities/obsidian|Obsidian]]** is the IDE, the LLM agent is the programmer, and the wiki is the codebase.
- **Three-Layer Architecture**: Detailed in **[[wiki/synthesis/llm-wiki-architecture|LLM Wiki Architecture]]**.
- **Paradigmatic Shift**: Contrast explored in **[[wiki/comparisons/rag-vs-llm-wiki|RAG vs. LLM Wiki]]**.

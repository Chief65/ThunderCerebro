# Obsidian

**Obsidian** is a local-first, markdown-based personal knowledge management (PKM) application that utilizes wikilinks and dynamic graph visualizations.

---

## Role in the LLM Wiki Pattern
- **Knowledge IDE**: Serves as the interactive viewing and navigation interface while the LLM acts as the autonomous maintainer.
- **Graph View**: Visualizes knowledge hubs, clusters, and orphan nodes.
- **Ecosystem Plugins**:
  - **Dataview**: Runs dynamic queries against YAML frontmatter.
  - **Marp**: Renders slide decks directly from markdown notes.
  - **Web Clipper**: Captures web articles cleanly into the `raw/` directory.
